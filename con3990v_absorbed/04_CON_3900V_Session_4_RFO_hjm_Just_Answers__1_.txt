Physical Completion

deliveries done, perf all services, all options expired (no longer cited). 
• What are two reasons a physically complete contract can’t be closed?in litigation or 
termination actions not complete (FAR 4.803-2(d).
Closeout Timelines
Per Table 4-2 in FAR 4.308-2(b), contract files must be closed as follows:
• Contracts using simplified acquisition procedures are considered closed when the contracting 
officer receives evidence of receipt of Property and Final Payment
• FFP contracts, other than those using SAP should be closed within 6 Months after the date 
on which the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion
• Contracts requiring settlement of indirect costs rates should be closed within 36 Months of 
the month the contracting officer receives evidence of physical completion
• All other contracts must be close within 20 Months of the contracting officer receiving 
evidence of physical completion
